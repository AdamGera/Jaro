# Requirements Document

## Introduction

Jard users can speak into the active app on Apple silicon macOS using the Rumble2 Flash model already installed for Soundfolio. The existing app menu and workspace stay familiar. A guided setup teaches the shortcut, walks through macOS permissions, and lets the user practice the real workflow.

## Glossary

- **Destination** means the foreground app and insertion point at the start of a take.
- **Take** means the audio captured during a single dictation.
- **HUD** means the floating recording and processing indicator.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1 · Guided first run

**User Story:** As a new user, I want to see dictation in action and grant its permissions one at a time so I can learn it without leaving Jard's workflow.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a native user opens Jard for the first time, Jard SHALL offer a skippable tour with an animated illustration of the shortcut and text insertion.
2. WHEN access is missing, Jard SHALL explain why dictation needs Microphone and Accessibility, show the appropriate macOS prompt or settings pane on request, and update the displayed state when access changes.
3. WHEN grants and the local model are usable, Jard SHALL offer a real shortcut practice field, show listening and processing states, and let users retry or skip practice.
4. WHEN setup ends, Jard SHALL explain how to dictate in any app, offer optional launch at login, and let users reopen the tour from existing Settings. A temporary interruption SHALL preserve the current setup step.
5. WHEN the user returns from System Settings and Accessibility is still unavailable, Jard SHALL offer to reset this installed copy's Accessibility entry and reopen the grant instructions. The reset SHALL require an explicit user action and apply only to Accessibility for Jard's current bundle identifier. WHEN the granted shortcut cannot start, Jard SHALL offer a guarded restart. Reopening SHALL resume the current setup step with the same app and library and protect active recordings.
6. WHEN macOS starts Jard at login and dictation is ready, Jard SHALL start dictation without showing its workspace. WHEN setup or the shortcut listener still needs attention, Jard SHALL show the workspace. Missing models and engine startup failures SHALL reveal the existing model setup and Retry screens. A later Dock reopen or app-switcher activation SHALL restore the retained workspace. App-switcher activation SHALL respect a minimized workspace and any other Jard window that already has focus.
7. WHEN setup, Dictation settings, or the CLI reads or changes Launch at Login, Jaro SHALL call ServiceManagement off the workspace and shortcut thread. Polling SHALL share the active operation. During a slow change the workspace SHALL reflect the newest click and reconcile with macOS when it answers. IF macOS requires approval, setup and Settings SHALL offer to open Login Items. Errors SHALL preserve the actual registration state.
8. WHEN Jaro launches normally with Show Dock icon off and setup ready, it SHALL start in the menu bar without opening the workspace. An ordinary launch SHALL show the workspace if setup or the shortcut listener needs attention. WHEN `jardctl start --hidden` launches Jaro, it SHALL remain in the menu bar even during setup while the status menu is available. If the menu is unavailable, Jaro SHALL show the workspace, including when quiet login had been selected before the engine was ready. Starting while Jaro is already running SHALL leave its visible state alone.
9. WHEN dictation setup requests access, Jaro SHALL show Microphone first if it is missing and Accessibility after the microphone is granted. Existing grants SHALL bypass their prompts. If practice or the completion screen is saved and dictation becomes unavailable, setup SHALL present the current recovery action and keep the saved step. Restoring permissions, the listener, and model readiness SHALL return the user to that step without replaying setup.
10. WHEN an explicit Accessibility request from setup becomes usable, Jaro SHALL bring the current setup window forward once if System Settings is still in front. It SHALL allow up to three seconds for an authentication sheet to close. The return SHALL expire if the user closes Settings, comes back to Jaro, defers setup, or the session becomes unavailable. A hidden or minimized workspace and an active capture or restart SHALL prevent activation.
11. WHEN an explicit Screen Recording or Mac audio Settings visit yields a grant for the current request, Jaro SHALL use the same guarded foreground return for the open workspace. A Screen Recording grant that needs a restart SHALL show the existing restart choice. A Screen Recording fallback SHALL leave the audio-only Settings visit waiting for its own grant. Native monitoring SHALL work while the workspace is inactive and retire after completion, dismissal, or a superseding visit.

### Requirement 2 · Global dictation

**User Story:** As a macOS user, I want to start and finish a take from my keyboard so I can dictate wherever I write.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN dictation is enabled and Jard is running, a configurable system-wide shortcut SHALL support hold, hold or double-tap to lock, and tap to start/stop with Jard's window closed.
2. WHEN a take is active, Jard SHALL display a responsive meter, the listening or processing state, and optional sound cues.
3. WHEN a take ends, Jard SHALL transcribe with the local Rumble2 Flash model and selected language hints. The pipeline SHALL leave existing notes and meeting capture intact.
4. WHEN audio is empty, startup fails, a permission is revoked, or another capture owns the recorder, Jard SHALL explain the outcome and make the next take available.
5. WHEN the selected shortcut is a standalone modifier, ordinary keyboard chords SHALL keep their usual behavior. Shortcut recording SHALL support documented modifier, function key, and modifier-chord choices.
6. WHEN the user selects Tap mode, Jard SHALL offer a configurable Cancel shortcut with Escape as the default. The shortcut SHALL discard only the active tap recording and consume its press, repeats, and release. WHEN a hold has lasted at least 300 ms and the shortcut remains down, Escape SHALL discard that take. While idle, double-tap locked, or delivering accepted dictations, Jard SHALL leave Escape available to the active app. An active annotation gesture retains priority for Escape.
7. WHEN an unrelated key or click turns an early shortcut press into a chord, Jard SHALL abandon the gesture and leave that input available to the app or macOS. A modifier-only hold SHALL also discard on a later ordinary key, and SHALL keep recording through Return or keypad Enter once held for 300 ms. WHEN macOS permits observing a chord consumed before the session tap, Jard SHALL use that evidence without recording or transmitting keyboard input. WHEN a release is lost during an app switch, Jard SHALL repair shortcut bookkeeping without starting or ending a recording from a physical-state poll.
8. WHEN an ordinary Hold release arrives before 300 ms, Jard SHALL discard the provisional take without submitting audio to the model or writing History. WHEN the second complete press and release is within 300 ms of the first release, a double-tap mode SHALL lock on the second release. In Hold or double-tap mode, a longer second press SHALL remain eligible for Hold. Jard SHALL preserve the beginning of the take by opening the recorder before the hold threshold and using its existing pre-roll when available.
9. WHEN the primary shortcut is a lone Option, Command, Control, or Shift key, Jard SHALL let users choose Left, Either, or Right. A fixed side SHALL start only on a fresh press of the chosen key by itself. Either SHALL accept a fresh press from either side and treat pressing both sides as a single gesture until both release. Existing fixed-side shortcuts SHALL keep their selected side on upgrade. The control SHALL be hidden for Fn and keyed chords.
10. WHEN the user records a dictation or raw dictation shortcut using only modifiers, Jard SHALL save the peak set of keys as the chord. Distinct modifier families SHALL work from either side, and a family recorded with both keys SHALL require both to be pressed within 80 ms of each other. The chord SHALL start on the edge that completes its exact modifier set and end when any required modifier falls out of that set. A conflicting binding SHALL leave both saved shortcuts intact.
11. WHEN Jard accepts a held modifier-only shortcut, a Tap press that survives 40 ms, or a modifier press that finishes a locked dictation, it SHALL prevent that same press from reading as a tap to other global observers. The app SHALL verify the configured modifiers are still held and consume its own synthetic event before it reaches the foreground app. Ordinary taps, Fn bindings, and keyed shortcuts SHALL continue through their existing paths.
12. WHEN Input Monitoring provides raw modifier state and macOS reports a remapped Caps Lock as a sided modifier, Jard SHALL use the physical side for fixed-side matching. Recording a remapped lone modifier SHALL capture it without a fixed side. Jard SHALL fall back to the Quartz flags when raw state is unavailable and SHALL reuse existing Input Monitoring consent.
13. WHEN Fn/Globe is part of a dictation binding, its companion Globe key event SHALL pass through without cancelling the gesture or invalidating its insertion target. Unrelated keys SHALL continue to affect shortcut matching and focus tracking. WHEN AppKit has no printable label for a recorded dead key, Jard SHALL use that key's printable label from the current macOS keyboard layout.
14. WHEN recording Paste Last or Dictate raw text, unmodified Delete or Forward Delete SHALL turn that binding off. A Delete key with a modifier SHALL record a chord, and an unmodified Delete key in a required recorder SHALL leave its binding intact. Restoring a default SHALL use the existing conflict checks. A conflict SHALL preserve the bindings and reopen the corresponding recorder when it is available.
15. WHEN a system shortcut absorbs a release, Jard SHALL let the next valid press rearm dictation or finish a hands-free take. It SHALL distinguish physically held keys from stale session state, require a neutral keyboard for background repair, and ignore late edges from before recovery. A background repair SHALL preserve any hands-free take until a real shortcut press arrives.
16. WHEN the selected keyboard layout changes, Jard SHALL resolve the ASCII letter shown on each shortcut again, including existing dictation bindings and the meeting and annotation defaults. Modifier-only keys, non-letter keys and letters absent from the layout SHALL keep their recorded codes. A layout change SHALL preserve active takes and the release of any shortcut Jard has already consumed.
17. WHEN macOS disables or invalidates a global event tap, Jaro SHALL recover it automatically while the listener is wanted and the necessary permissions remain granted. A gap in the session tap SHALL fence earlier input and discard an unfinished shortcut gesture. Released dictations and accepted output SHALL continue. Loss of either auxiliary tap SHALL keep the session listener available. App Nap SHALL leave the active listener responsive while macOS remains free to sleep.
18. WHEN the user opens Shortcut mode, Jaro SHALL illustrate the key presses and recording intervals for Hold, Hold or double-tap, Tap, and Double-tap. It SHALL show the current binding and selected mode, save through the existing preference, and allow keyboard selection and dismissal with focus returned to the invoking control. Reduce Motion SHALL present a static diagram of each gesture.

### Requirement 3 · Insertion and recovery

**User Story:** As a writer, I want fresh dictation at the current caret and a way to recover a take if insertion fails.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a fresh take is ready in Paste mode, Jaro SHALL use the app and caret active at delivery. Ordinary clipboard paste SHALL work even when macOS Accessibility cannot provide a stable text element, window, or selection. Jaro SHALL post the Command modifier and V key edges and preserve the prior clipboard and newer clipboard edits.
2. IF the active app changes while the paste is being prepared or the current field uses secure input, Jaro SHALL prevent automatic insertion. Explicit typing and recovery actions SHALL continue validating the field they captured. Completed text SHALL remain available through Paste Last or local history.
3. WHEN the user invokes Paste Last, Jard SHALL use the currently focused app. Copy-only SHALL put text on the clipboard for manual use.
4. WHEN history is enabled, Jard SHALL keep searchable original and final text, app name, and date on the Mac for the selected retention period and allow deleting entries.
5. WHEN output settings are enabled, Jard SHALL support vocabulary hints and corrections for frequent names, optional filler removal and spoken line breaks, and different insertion behavior by app or website.
6. WHEN paste targets a Windows App or Microsoft Remote Desktop client, Jard SHALL type with paced key events mapped through the active keyboard layout. WHEN paste targets an app embedding Chromium 155 or 156, Jard SHALL type in bounded Unicode bursts. Both paths SHALL keep the clipboard intact and translate line breaks to Shift–Return. A secure input change, focus change, or cancellation SHALL stop remaining text and leave the full take available for recovery.
7. WHEN a paste is about to post Command–V, Jard SHALL recheck that it still owns the clipboard after checking focus through Accessibility. A clipboard edit recognized during that check SHALL be preserved.
8. WHEN macOS Secure Event Input withholds keyboard events from the shortcut tap, Jard SHALL recover compatible annotation and paste-recovery chords through temporary macOS hotkeys. Returning to regular input SHALL release those registrations and balance any held shortcut. The recovery path SHALL apply the same secure-field and focus checks before delivering any text.
9. WHEN macOS advertises automatically translated clipboard formats alongside original formats on the same item, Jard SHALL preserve the originals without reading the generated alternatives. It SHALL restore the original items in order with their visible format flags. IF an original format is unreadable or the snapshot exceeds the preservation limit, Jard SHALL leave the clipboard intact and keep the dictation available for recovery.
10. WHEN successive dictations or Paste Last reach the same insertion point without intervening input, Jard SHALL join them with sentence-aware capitalization and spacing. It SHALL preserve likely proper names, avoid extra spaces before closing punctuation or after opening brackets, and leave the saved transcript independent of its insertion context. Typing, clicks, focus or caret changes, and standalone output SHALL break the continuation.
11. WHEN Jard posts Command–V for dictation, Paste Last, a meeting excerpt, or an annotation, it SHALL resolve V from the active layout's Command layer, then its plain layer, then the plain layer of the current ASCII-capable layout. A layout change SHALL apply to the next paste without relaunch. Clipboard ownership SHALL still be checked immediately before posting.
12. WHEN successive clipboard pastes are ready, Jaro SHALL release the next output after a 100 ms paste settle window and restore the clipboard 500 ms after the latest owned paste. A paste that still owns the preceding temporary clipboard SHALL retain the original clipboard snapshot. A newer clipboard write SHALL take precedence. Explicit copy SHALL end the pending restoration and start a fresh insertion context.
13. WHEN dictation is enabled, Jaro SHALL offer Paste Last Dictation from its existing status menu. The item SHALL show the saved shortcut when AppKit can display it and remain usable if the shortcut is unbound. It SHALL paste through the existing focus and clipboard checks after menu tracking ends.
14. WHEN browsing History, a mouse drag between visible rows SHALL select the inclusive range, with adjacent rows sharing a highlight. Clicking, Shift-clicking, Command-clicking, and keyboard selection SHALL continue to work. A row action SHALL copy that row's final text without changing the selection. Copying from the detail card SHALL show feedback once the native clipboard action succeeds.

### Requirement 4 · macOS reference parity

**User Story:** As the owner of Jard, I want a record of how Hex handles each macOS dictation behavior so we can verify parity and keep extending the integration.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN documenting the reference, the project SHALL identify onboarding steps, permission states, shortcut and HUD behavior, model-specific constraints, and differences between released features and Labs.
2. WHEN marking a parity item complete, the task list SHALL distinguish implementation, automated verification, and physical Mac verification.
3. WHEN a Hex behavior requires a different speech model or service, Jard SHALL preserve Rumble2 Flash and record the outstanding adaptation.
4. WHEN the user opens Find settings from desktop Settings or Dictation, Jard SHALL search the names, help, and group names of available controls. Choosing a result SHALL reveal and focus its control. Escape and outside dismissal SHALL restore focus. The shortcut SHALL respect open dialogs, shortcut capture, and active editors. Settings search SHALL leave preference values unchanged and exclude user-entered content.
5. WHEN a stored dictation preference has an invalid value, Jard SHALL load its default and keep valid sibling fields. Invalid parts of one shortcut SHALL recover as a unit. Loading SHALL preserve the saved preference and history. An explicit workspace or CLI save SHALL preserve the original malformed dictionary before writing a repair, keep unfamiliar fields, and retain unrelated edits. A stored value that is not a dictionary SHALL prevent saving over it.
6. WHEN saved dictation settings change while Jard is running, Jard SHALL detect the change off the input thread and adopt the settings when idle. A take and its held shortcut SHALL finish with their current settings. A failed read SHALL keep the active settings and block writes until Jard adopts a readable snapshot. A late read SHALL leave a newer local save intact.

### Requirement 4A · Play recent dictation audio

**User Story:** As a macOS user, I want to listen to recent dictation audio from History to check what the microphone captured.

#### Acceptance criteria

1. WHEN History is enabled and a new dictation is saved, Jaro SHALL keep the original microphone WAV locally and offer Play in its History detail pane while the clip is available.
2. WHEN 24 hours elapse from capture, Jaro SHALL remove the audio and keep the transcript under its existing policy. Jaro SHALL clean up at launch and at the next deadline while open.
3. WHEN an entry is deleted, cleared, or pruned, Jaro SHALL stop any playback for it and remove its clip. Older entries and entries with missing or expired audio SHALL omit Play.
4. IF saving or playback fails, dictation, transcript retention, and insertion SHALL continue through their existing paths.

### Requirement 5 · Microphone lifecycle and overlapping takes

**User Story:** As a frequent dictation user, I want consistent speech boundaries and to begin another take while earlier speech is being decoded.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN Keep microphone ready is enabled and macOS permits recording, Jard SHALL share a warm microphone across takes, retain only bounded in-memory pre-roll while idle, and release the device when disabled, asleep, signed out, or handing over to a meeting.
2. WHEN on-demand capture is selected, Jard SHALL release the microphone after each take. In both modes finishing with the shortcut SHALL use its event timestamp and retain up to 200 ms for the last syllable. Sustained speech at the end of that window SHALL extend the tail until a 160 ms pause or a 600 ms cap. An explicit command-line stop or secure-input interruption SHALL end capture immediately. Cancellation SHALL avoid output and release the stage.
3. WHEN a new shortcut press arrives during the previous take's release tail, Jard SHALL preserve its press time, destination, and preferences and open the next take when the recorder is available. A warm microphone with pre-roll enabled SHALL retain up to 450 ms before the new press and the delivered audio since the press. An on-demand take or a take that suppresses playback SHALL start with live audio once the recorder is available. WHEN a take is finalized, Jard SHALL allow a new take while earlier jobs use the same Rumble2 Flash engine. Jobs SHALL keep their original destination, mode, settings, and cancellation independently. Automatic insertions SHALL preserve submission order and wait until active recording has ended.
4. WHEN a destination is unavailable or a recording ends unexpectedly, Jard SHALL keep recoverable text and avoid automatic insertion until an intentional paste. Feedback SHALL identify listening, pending output, failure, or completion.
5. WHEN a global dictation starts, Jard SHALL prevent idle system sleep for that take. It SHALL release the assertion when the microphone take ends, including on failure or cancellation.
6. WHEN the user chooses Mute or Lower audio under While dictating, Jard SHALL change the current Mac output during each global take. Lower audio SHALL apply the selected percentage to each controlled channel to retain its balance. Jard SHALL restore the original device when the take ends and preserve volume changes made outside the take. Warm takes with either option SHALL exclude audio captured before the take began. Do nothing SHALL remain the default.
7. WHEN the user chooses Pause media under While dictating, Jard SHALL pause supported running players that were playing and resume the same playback after capture only if it is still paused. Browser playback control follows the Labs scope.
8. WHEN Jard needs Automation consent to control Music, Spotify, or VLC, it SHALL ask only from the explicit Dictation settings action. Capture SHALL continue when a player is closed, denied, or unresponsive. Replacing the player process, changing the track or position, or resuming manually SHALL prevent Jard from sending a stale resume command.
9. WHEN a finalized native microphone take contains only near-silence, Jard SHALL report no speech, skip model inference and output processing, and release the staged audio. The last usable transcript and History SHALL remain available. A sustained quiet take that rises above its own noise floor SHALL reach the existing Rumble2 Flash pipeline.
10. WHEN the installed Rumble2 Flash alignment head shows a confident absence of acoustic tokens in a finalized native microphone take, Jard SHALL skip text generation. For a shortcut finish, the level and acoustic screens SHALL determine eligibility from audio up to the event timestamp. The full retained tail SHALL stay available for transcription once the take passes. Ambiguous evidence or an unavailable alignment head SHALL allow transcription through the existing Rumble2 Flash path. Jard SHALL use its installed model weights for this decision.
11. WHEN the microphone disconnects or stalls during an intentional take, Jard SHALL stop at the last recorded sample and recover eligible speech from that audio. Jard SHALL process it using the original take's settings, preserve it in Paste Last and retained History, and copy it if the clipboard revision and secure-input state allow. Insertion SHALL require the user to paste. A plain shortcut take with only pre-roll or an audio discontinuity greater than 250 ms SHALL be discarded. For a reported device failure, intention SHALL be checked at the failure timestamp. For a stall detected later, the last valid audio timestamp SHALL establish the boundary.
12. WHEN an idle warm microphone stops sending audio for four seconds, becomes unavailable, or finishes a take containing only digital zero samples, Jard SHALL reopen it using the selected device policy and bounded retry delays. A stalled or disconnected take's completion SHALL release its capture so the next shortcut can start.
13. WHEN the cold microphone fails before the hold threshold while the shortcut remains held, Jard SHALL attempt to reopen it within the same gesture. The original destination, preferences, and pressed and released timestamps SHALL belong to the logical take. A release before readiness or cancellation SHALL abandon the result, and late events from the retired recorder SHALL leave the current attempt intact. Permission errors SHALL follow permission recovery.
14. WHEN the selected cold microphone is present but fails to open, Jard SHALL retry the selection twice at 100 ms intervals before trying the system input. If the selected microphone is absent or System default is selected, Jard SHALL try the system input immediately. The fallback SHALL honor the Bluetooth preference, report the device used for the take, and preserve the saved selection. A release or cancellation SHALL stop the pending retries. Permission errors SHALL remain in the permission flow.

15. WHEN a warm microphone delivers only digital zeros before the release of a finished take, Jaro SHALL try one clock renegotiation on the next open of that same input. Pre-roll, release-tail audio, and cancelled takes SHALL not control the decision. Jaro SHALL use an advertised alternate rate, restore the original rate before recording, and reopen normally when a rate reset is unavailable or the input is in use. An interrupted stream SHALL keep its ordinary reopen path.
16. WHEN a dictation exceeds Rumble2 Flash's 25-second input window, Jaro SHALL cut through the quietest 100 ms near the end of each window and retain every sample in order. The decoder SHALL retain existing multilingual pause splits and release-time speech screening. New mixed meeting jobs SHALL retain their window policy across restart. Older saved checkpoints SHALL continue to use the original boundaries.
17. WHEN a Hold gesture is still provisional, Jaro SHALL defer playback suppression, media pause, and its recording sleep assertion until the 300 ms hold threshold. A tap that commits a recording, or a command start, SHALL apply those effects when accepted. An unfinished or cancelled gesture SHALL release its effects and leave any previously accepted output intact.

18. WHEN a warm microphone cannot open or becomes unresponsive, Jaro SHALL keep the worker available and release a dead input before waiting. An initial open failure SHALL retry with a 250 ms starting delay capped at five seconds. A stream that fails before eight seconds of continuous packet delivery SHALL grow its retry delay across replacement opens, up to one minute. A steady stream SHALL reopen without waiting after failure and reset the retry delay. A new press or input route change SHALL allow an immediate attempt and retain any accumulated stream backoff. An unplugged saved input SHALL use the existing Bluetooth-aware default until the saved input returns.
19. WHEN the connected saved microphone fails to open in warm mode, Jaro SHALL record from the Bluetooth-aware system input if it is available and retain the saved selection. The worker SHALL retry the saved input from standby with bounded delays and keep the working fallback until its replacement opens. A take, including its release tail, SHALL stay on the input it started with. Recording feedback SHALL identify a fallback.

20. WHEN the MacBook lid closes, warm and on-demand dictation SHALL skip the unavailable internal microphone and use the eligible system input or a physical stand-in. Jaro SHALL leave a wired headphone input eligible and preserve explicit external device selections. Virtual, aggregate, and Continuity inputs SHALL be used only when chosen or set as the system default. The Bluetooth preference SHALL prefer an available physical alternative and allow the Bluetooth default when it is the only usable input. If only the closed-lid microphone is available, Jaro SHALL explain how to restore input. Closing the lid during a take on the internal microphone SHALL use the existing interruption recovery. The System default choice in Dictation settings SHALL show the resolved input for the current Bluetooth preference.

21. WHEN a warm input is opening, Jaro SHALL continue to accept capture controls. A take SHALL begin on the working fallback while a preferred device is being tested. If there is no ready input, Stop, Cancel, or a shortcut release before audio is available SHALL abandon that take. Late open results SHALL respect the current route and preserve an active take on its existing input. The next capture SHALL still be available.

22. WHEN the warm microphone has no usable input, Dictation settings SHALL show the current problem without requiring a failed take. A working alternate input SHALL be identified while the saved input is unavailable. The notice SHALL update on recovery or route changes and clear when the warm worker is retired. A late event from a retired worker or preference request SHALL leave the current status intact. Last-take feedback SHALL retain its existing lifecycle.

23. WHEN the Mac locks, sleeps, turns off its display, or switches to another user's session, Jaro SHALL release the dictation microphone and suspend shortcut admission. Jaro SHALL read the initial display and lock state before opening the microphone at launch. It SHALL resume dictation and, if enabled, warm standby only after every active hold clears.

24. WHEN the Dictation microphone selector is visible, Jaro SHALL refresh its available devices and resolved System default after device, default-input, Core Audio restart, and lid changes. The selector SHALL keep an unavailable saved selection, identify its fallback when available, and explain a closed lid when it blocks the only input. Route-list refresh SHALL leave the running recorder and saved settings alone. A refresh invalidated by newer device information SHALL yield to the newer snapshot.

### Requirement 6 · Output profiles and controls

**User Story:** As someone who dictates into different apps, I want to control how the words are transformed and delivered.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN an output profile applies, Jard SHALL apply its enabled steps in order, support a preview, and allow defaults, app rules, and website overrides.
2. WHEN requested, Jard SHALL offer a second raw dictation binding, a configurable Paste Last binding, and independent history controls including usage totals. Duplicate bindings SHALL be rejected.
3. WHEN offering optional processing or developer features, Jard SHALL preserve the Rumble2 Flash speech engine. User-selected external scripts SHALL run only on explicit opt-in, with bounded execution and a recoverable failure path. Features tied to a different model or service SHALL be mapped to a local equivalent or tracked with a specific compatibility gap.
4. WHEN exposing optional feedback, Jard SHALL offer sound styles and HUD positions, including an insertion-point option where macOS exposes a position.
5. WHEN a regular dictation matches an output profile, its vocabulary SHALL precede shared terms, with case-insensitive deduplication and the Rumble2 Flash term limit. Its rewrite instructions SHALL override the shared instructions when supplied. Local Qwen SHALL receive the effective vocabulary when rewriting. Raw dictation SHALL bypass the profile.
6. WHEN editing a profile, the user SHALL be able to choose its scope, order its steps, set its vocabulary and rewrite instructions, and preview the matched profile. Saving SHALL validate the definition without performing a rewrite or executing a script. Previously saved profiles SHALL keep their matching and processing behavior.
7. WHEN a profile uses local rewriting, the user SHALL be able to bound each rewrite with a deadline. IF a rewrite fails or times out, Jard SHALL keep the text that entered that rewrite and continue with subsequent steps. IF a group of adjacent transformations fails, Jard SHALL discard the partial changes from that group and continue from its input. The recovered text SHALL use the configured delivery path with the existing destination and clipboard checks.
8. WHEN a profile includes word replacements, Jard SHALL apply shared replacements first and the profile's own replacements second. The profile editor SHALL allow replacing or deleting a phrase. Preview and dictation SHALL use the same correction rules, including the selected scope.
9. WHEN the input to a rewrite consists only of digits or English number words with numeric joiners and punctuation, Jard SHALL skip local Qwen and continue with the remaining ordered steps. Mixed speech SHALL still use the configured rewrite.
10. WHEN a user adds Lowercase, SpongeBob case, or No trailing punctuation to an output profile, preview and dictation SHALL run these local steps in their selected order. Alternating case SHALL advance only on alphabetic characters. Removing trailing punctuation SHALL remove the final run of sentence marks while preserving internal punctuation, closing quotes and brackets, and whitespace passed to later steps.
11. WHEN a website rule or preview context contains a bare host, URL, port, trailing dot, or internationalized hostname, Jard SHALL compare canonical hosts. Existing wildcard rules SHALL continue to match only subdomains. Application rules SHALL also accept the Finder bundle name, with or without the .app extension. Website rules SHALL retain priority and the first matching rule of each scope SHALL win.
12. WHEN a normal dictation finishes, Jard SHALL pin the foreground application for output profile selection at the logical release or explicit stop, including a take released during another take's tail. A website profile SHALL use the focused page from that release when it can be read within one second. IF the page is unavailable, secure, changed during the read, or too slow, Jard SHALL use app or default matching without reusing an earlier page. The original insertion destination, delivery safeguards, and preferences SHALL remain associated with the take. Raw and practice takes SHALL bypass profile context reads.
13. WHEN a website profile needs the active page of a supported browser, Jard SHALL read its active tab using existing Automation access and fall back to Accessibility if unavailable. Website matching and local rewriting SHALL use only the HTTP or HTTPS host. Background lookup SHALL leave the browser running as it was and SHALL NOT prompt for consent. The user SHALL be able to request Automation for the running browser explicitly from Dictation settings.
14. WHEN users open Dictation Output, Jaro SHALL show the saved global and scoped steps in order. Testing a typed sample SHALL use the saved default corrections and built-in steps. Rewrite and script steps SHALL be skipped in this test. Edit with agent SHALL copy instructions for the local settings CLI, and Reveal settings file SHALL select the existing preferences file in Finder.
15. WHEN the running app receives a vocabulary list, add, or remove request through `jardctl`, Jaro SHALL use the same shared dictionary as Settings. Adding an existing term SHALL merge new alternate spellings case-insensitively and report an unchanged request. Removing a term SHALL remove its alternate spellings without modifying independent corrections. Regular dictation and preview SHALL replace saved whole alternate phrases with the preferred spelling. Raw dictation SHALL bypass those corrections.
16. WHEN users open Vocabulary in Dictation settings, Jaro SHALL show the saved count and an editor for terms and comma-separated alternate spellings. It SHALL focus a blank term when empty or adding a row, save valid edits after a typing pause, flush them on close, and preserve unrelated changes from the CLI. Row actions SHALL stay attached to their original rows after deletion. The editor SHALL explain the existing Rumble2 Flash limits and keep overflowing text available for correction.
17. WHEN a profile uses a local executable script, Jaro SHALL require explicit approval for the selected version in Output. If the file changes, is replaced, or has no recorded approval, Jaro SHALL preserve usable dictation and ask for approval again. The script runner SHALL execute the approved file contents, keep the existing time and output limits, and skip scripts in previews and raw dictation.
18. WHEN users select a local transformation catalog, Jaro SHALL list its named transformations and allow profile steps to reference them in order. Approval SHALL fingerprint the catalog and every declared entry file and dependency. IF a dependency changes or a named step fails, Jaro SHALL keep the text entering its transformation stage. Preview and raw dictation SHALL skip named external transformations.
19. WHEN Jaro offers an embedded JavaScript or TypeScript transformation host, it SHALL review the configured entry points and pinned import graph, keep foreground context limited to the approved API, and grant program and network capabilities explicitly. An unavailable import or denied capability SHALL leave the previous text available for delivery.
20. WHEN a user creates a transformation workspace from Output or the local CLI, Jaro SHALL provide a working config and editor types while preserving existing files. Output SHALL discover the default workspace without replacing a selected script. WHEN a config or import is edited, review SHALL refresh its definitions or load error. A failed edit SHALL retain the last valid labels for reference and SHALL require a successful review before approving the edited version.
21. WHEN a macOS configuration profile disables transformations, Jaro SHALL leave configured transformations saved, skip built-in and custom steps, and leave word replacements and local rewriting available. WHEN the profile restricts transformations to loopback, Jaro SHALL refuse programs and connect only to validated loopback addresses, including on redirects. Unrestricted requests SHALL use the configured proxy and macOS TLS trust. Output SHALL display the active managed restriction.
22. WHEN a transformation explicitly uses the console during an approved take, or its configuration logs during a manual CLI check, Jaro SHALL record the message in the private local log. The workspace and setup SHALL remain reachable if the status item is unavailable, and opening the workspace SHALL restore the Dock icon while the window is visible.
13. WHEN a user selects an interface language, Jard SHALL apply it independently of the transcription and recording language. System SHALL follow the Mac's preferred interface language with a fallback for unsupported locales. The preference SHALL apply to workspace controls, onboarding, and native controls.

   WHEN Jaro displays meeting and calendar clock times or writes meeting Markdown, English SHALL use 12-hour time and the other interface languages SHALL use 24-hour time. Saved meetings SHALL use the local recording start when available and fall back to their creation time. JSON timestamps, transcript and subtitle offsets, and speech language hints SHALL retain their existing formats and values.
14. WHEN the dictation indicator is visible, Jaro SHALL keep it available on the active macOS Space, including another application's fullscreen Space. A Dock activation policy change SHALL refresh the indicator's Space behavior without revealing a hidden indicator. Recovery SHALL preserve animation and focus, confirm moves on a later tick, and stop retrying after a bounded sequence. Top and Bottom SHALL follow the pointer's display and keep the rendered plate clear of its menu bar, camera housing, and Dock. Cursor placement SHALL retain its existing caret anchor.
15. WHEN a shortcut press can start dictation with Keep microphone ready enabled, Jaro SHALL prepare the selected start cue off the input thread and leave a 40 ms grace period for ordinary chords. A discarded press SHALL withdraw a pending cue or fade a sounding cue over 20 ms. An on-demand take SHALL play its start cue only when the microphone is ready and the capture is intentional. An interrupted recorder open, next press, or cancelled take SHALL leave each cue owned by its original gesture.
16. WHEN users choose a sound style, Jaro SHALL offer Classic, Signature, Click, Soft tone, Wood, Warm synth, and Thock, with Classic as the default. Previously saved Soft and Glass selections SHALL still play their existing macOS sounds. The existing audio settings SHALL let users audition sample dictation, start, stop, completion when available, and failure from any style at their chosen volume before saving their choice. Preview SHALL stop when dismissed or when a real recording starts. Entering the setup ready step SHALL play the style's success cue once per tour.
17. WHEN a keyboard dictation successfully delivers text, Jaro SHALL play a separate completion cue only for styles that contain one and only after two seconds have elapsed since the stop cue. A stop cue waiting for audio restoration, a failed or cancelled output, Paste Last, a meeting excerpt, or a command capture SHALL produce no completion cue. Preview sounds SHALL leave real dictation timing untouched.
18. WHEN users audition a sound with the indicator visible, Jaro SHALL show the matching listening, processing, completion, or failure state using the compact indicator inside Settings. A preview SHALL use sample audio levels and honor Reduce Motion. The Hidden indicator setting SHALL leave audio previews available.
19. WHEN users open Sound style, Jaro SHALL show a single sheet with the saved style, a play control for each style, and the individual cues supported by the style being auditioned. Selecting a style SHALL save it and play its sample. The separate play control SHALL leave the saved style intact. Explicit auditions SHALL play at 50% when live cues are disabled or set to zero. Replacing or dismissing an audition SHALL fade its active audio over 20 ms and cancel pending cues.
20. WHEN a user reads or changes `ui_language` or `show_dock_icon` through the authenticated CLI, Jaro SHALL use the same supported choices and saved values as the workspace. A settings batch SHALL validate all requested changes before saving, including in offline apply. Changes to Interface language SHALL update the workspace selection and native controls, including when the selected and System choices resolve to the same language. Home and End SHALL select the first and last available results only when the search field is empty. Search text SHALL keep its native editing keys.
